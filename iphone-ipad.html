Self-Help Guide

iPhone & iPad
Troubleshooting

Frozen screen, storage full, iCloud not syncing? Most iPhone and iPad issues have simple fixes — try these before calling anyone.

Fix it yourself

Common iPhone & iPad Problems

1

Force restart your device

iPhone 8 and later / iPad with Face ID: Quickly press and release Volume Up → Volume Down → hold Side button until the Apple logo appears.
Older iPhones / iPads with Home button: Hold Home + Side/Top button for 10 seconds. This fixes most freezes and glitches without deleting anything.

2

Check for iOS/iPadOS updates

Go to Settings → General → Software Update. Apple regularly fixes bugs in updates. Many issues — battery drain, app crashes, sync problems — are already fixed and you just need to update.

3

Free up storage space

Go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage. iOS will recommend things to offload. Delete apps you don't use, clear large photos/videos to iCloud, and offload apps. When storage is nearly full, your phone slows down and apps crash.

4

Sign out and back into iCloud

If photos, contacts, or notes aren't syncing, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Sign Out (choose to keep data on device), then sign back in. This refreshes your iCloud connection and fixes most sync issues.

5

Reset network settings for Wi-Fi / cellular issues

Go to Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings. This clears all saved Wi-Fi passwords and VPN settings (you'll need to re-enter Wi-Fi passwords), but fixes most connection problems.

6

Backup and restore as last resort

Back up to iCloud (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup → Back Up Now) or to your Mac/PC using Finder/iTunes. Then go to Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Erase All Content and Settings. Restore from your backup. This fixes deep software issues.

Tips & Hacks

Get More From Your iPhone

💰

Save Money on iPhones — No Proof Required

Apple's Education Store gives real discounts to anyone — they don't verify if you're actually a student or teacher. Also always check the Apple Certified Refurbished Store for iPhones at $100+ off with full Apple warranty. Same phone, different price tag.

📸

iCloud Photos = Free backup (5 GB)

Turn on Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Photos. Your photos back up automatically. Need more space? 50 GB is $0.99/month. Way cheaper than losing your photos.

🔋

Battery draining fast? Check Background Refresh

Go to Settings → General → Background App Refresh and turn it off for apps that don't need it. Also check Settings → Battery to see which apps are using the most power.

🔐

Lost your iPhone? Find My is your best friend

Make sure Settings → [Your Name] → Find My → Find My iPhone is ON before you lose it. Then you can track, lock, or erase it from icloud.com/find.

🌐

Can't access iCloud? Use the web version

Everything in iCloud — photos, files, contacts, notes, email — is also accessible at icloud.com from any browser, even if your phone isn't working.

Transfer to a new iPhone for free

When you get a new iPhone, hold it next to your old one and use Quick Start — it transfers everything wirelessly. No iTunes, no computer needed. Works with iOS 12.4 and newer.
