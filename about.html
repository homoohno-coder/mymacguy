The short version

A career spent making technology get out of the way.

Before mymacguy.net was a business it was a habit. The pivot to full-time independent work came after seven years running operations and IT inside Innovative Artists — a Beverly Hills talent agency where the technology had to be invisible and bulletproof. Agents don't have time for a ticket system, and clients don't reschedule because your email is down.

That's the standard I bring to every house and every small business: senior-level work, communicated like an adult. I tell you what I'm doing, what it'll cost, and what to do if it happens again. I write things down. I follow up.

Today the practice is deliberately small. One technician, by design — so the person who answers the phone is the person who actually fixes the problem.

How I work

Four commitments.

01

Data first.

Before I touch anything risky, your data is safe — verified, off-device, recoverable. Then I work.

02

Estimates verbally, in writing afterward.

You hear the cost before the meter starts. You get a written summary after, including what I changed and why.

03

No upsells. Ever.

I don't make money on hardware markup. If the right answer is "the machine you have is fine," that's what you'll hear.

04

If I can't help, you don't pay.

Some problems are someone else's specialty. I'll tell you, refer you, and you won't owe me for the visit.
