Can't sign in? Email not loading? Work through these steps yourself — most issues are fixable in minutes without calling anyone.

Start here

Can't Sign In? Try This First.

Before anything else: Check if Microsoft is having an outage — it happens more than you'd think. Visit status.office365.com or Downdetector. If there's a known issue, all you can do is wait.
1

Try signing in directly at Outlook.com or Office.com

Sometimes the desktop app has issues but the web version works fine. Go to outlook.com or office.com and try signing in there. If web works, your desktop app just needs to be reset.

2

Reset your Microsoft account password

Even if you think you know your password, a reset often fixes mysterious login failures. Go to the Microsoft Password Reset page, enter your email, and follow the steps to set a new password.

3

Try a different browser or use Incognito/Private mode

Extensions and cached data can block logins. Press Cmd + Shift + N (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + N (Windows) to open an Incognito window, then try signing in again.

4

Clear your browser cache and cookies

In Chrome or Edge, press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac). Select "All time" as the range, check Cookies and Cached images, then clear. Restart the browser and try again.

5

Check if your Microsoft 365 subscription is active

Sign in to account.microsoft.com/services to see your subscriptions. If it shows expired or payment failed, update your billing info. A lapsed subscription will lock you out of apps and email.

6

Remove and re-add your account in the Outlook app

On Mac: open Outlook → top menu → Tools → Accounts → click your account → remove it, then add it back fresh. On Windows: open Outlook → File → Account Settings → select account → Remove, then add again.

7

Check with your IT admin or account owner

If your Microsoft 365 account was set up by an employer, school, or IT department, your account may have been disabled, the license removed, or a password policy may be blocking you. Contact whoever manages your organization's Microsoft 365 account.

Tips & Hacks

Save Money & Work Smarter

💰

Apple Education Store — No Proof Required

Apple's Education Store gives real discounts on Macs and iPads to anyone — they do not verify enrollment. No student ID needed. Also check the Apple Certified Refurbished Store for $100–$200 off with full warranty.

🌐

Use Office Online for Free

You don't need a paid Microsoft 365 subscription to use Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Office.com gives you the web versions free with a free Microsoft account. Great for light use.

🖥️

Remote Help from Wahed

For Microsoft 365 setup, email migration, or account issues, Wahed can connect remotely to walk you through it. Click Zoho Assist to start a remote session.

🔒

Enable Microsoft Authenticator App

Download the free Microsoft Authenticator app on your iPhone. It makes logging into Microsoft 365 much more secure and you can approve sign-ins with one tap instead of entering a code.

📧

Outlook on Mac — Use the Web Version

The Outlook desktop app on Mac can be buggy. If it's crashing or not loading, Outlook Web (OWA) at outlook.com is just as full-featured and works in Safari. Many people prefer it.

📞

Microsoft Support — Direct Line

Microsoft's paid support line: 1-800-642-7676. For free community support: answers.microsoft.com. For business/admin issues, contact Microsoft directly through the Admin Center.
