Troubleshooting

Slow Mac, crashes, won't start? Work through these steps — most common Mac problems are fixable without spending a dime.

Fix it yourself

Common Mac Problems & Solutions

1

Restart your Mac properly

This solves more problems than anything else. Go to Apple menu → Restart (not just close the lid). A full restart clears memory, closes stuck processes, and applies pending updates. Do this first, always.

2

Check for and install macOS updates

Go to System Settings → General → Software Update. Many crashes and bugs are fixed in updates Apple has already released — you may just be running an old version.

3

Free up storage space

Go to System Settings → General → Storage. If you have less than 10–15 GB free, your Mac will slow to a crawl. Use the built-in recommendations to remove large files, empty trash, and clear caches. macOS needs breathing room to operate.

4

Run Disk Utility First Aid

Open Finder → Applications → Utilities → Disk Utility. Select your main drive (Macintosh HD), click First Aid, then Run. This checks and repairs file system errors that cause slowness and crashes.

5

Boot in Safe Mode to isolate problems

Apple Silicon Mac: Shut down → hold power button until startup options appear → hold Shift → click Continue in Safe Mode.
Intel Mac: Restart and immediately hold Shift. Safe Mode disables third-party startup items and often reveals if an app is causing the issue.

6

Run Apple Diagnostics to check hardware

Apple Silicon: Shut down → hold power until "Loading startup options" → hold Cmd + D.
Intel: Restart and hold D. This tests your RAM, logic board, and other hardware. If it reports an error code, look it up here.

7

Reset NVRAM (Intel Macs only)

Shut down your Mac. Turn it on and immediately hold Cmd + Option + P + R for about 20 seconds. Release after the second startup chime (or after the Apple logo appears twice). This clears display settings, boot disk selection, and other low-level settings.

Tips & Tricks

Make Your Mac Run Better

💰

Save $100–$200 on Apple Products

Before you buy any new Mac or accessory, always check the Apple Certified Refurbished Store. These are the same Apple products, factory reset, with a 1-year warranty and eligible for AppleCare — at $100–$200 off. You literally can't tell the difference from new. Also check Apple's Education store if you're a student, teacher, or work at a school.

⚡

Activity Monitor = Mac's Task Manager

Open Applications → Utilities → Activity Monitor. Sort by CPU or Memory to find what's hogging resources. You can force-quit any app right from there.

🧹

Free Cleanup: OnyX

OnyX is a trusted, free Mac maintenance tool. It clears system caches, rebuilds databases, and runs maintenance scripts. Use it when your Mac feels sluggish.

🦠

Free Malware Scanner: Malwarebytes

Malwarebytes for Mac is free to scan and remove adware, browser hijackers, and malware. Run it if your browser is acting weird or showing unexpected ads.

🖥️

Remote Help: Join.me / Zoho Assist

If Wahed needs to take a look remotely, he'll send you a link to Zoho Assist. Click it, share your screen, and he can help without being there in person.

📊

Check Battery Health

Hold Option and click the Battery icon in your menu bar to see battery condition. Or: System Settings → Battery. A "Service Recommended" warning means the battery needs replacement soon.

💾

Always Have a Backup (Time Machine)

Plug in an external drive and go to System Settings → General → Time Machine. Turn it on. If your Mac ever dies, you lose nothing. A 1TB external drive costs $50–$60 on Amazon.
