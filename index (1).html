Common questions

Before you call.

How do you charge?

Hourly, in 30-minute increments after the first hour. You get a verbal estimate before any work begins and a written summary after. No bench fees, no diagnostic fees, no parts markup beyond a small handling charge. If I can't help, you don't pay.

Do you come to my home or office?

Yes — most appointments are on-site across Greater Los Angeles. For quick issues I can also remote in via Zoho Assist (you keep watching the whole time) or walk you through it on the phone.

What if my Mac can't be repaired?

You get a clear opinion and the honest math: repair vs replace vs trade-in. If replacement is the right move, I'll source the right machine (often well below retail), set it up, migrate your data, and recycle the old one — all without an Apple Store visit.

Is remote support safe?

Yes. Sessions run through Zoho Assist with end-to-end encryption; you initiate them, you watch every action, and you can end the connection in one click. I never request access without you on the phone.

Do you work on Windows or Linux?

Apple is the focus. For mixed Mac + Windows small offices (Microsoft 365, shared printers, domain email, file servers) — yes, fluently. For a Windows-only environment, I'll refer you to a specialist.

What hours do you keep?

Most days 9am–7pm. Evenings and weekends by arrangement; emergencies handled when I can. Text is the fastest channel — (310) 980-1964.
