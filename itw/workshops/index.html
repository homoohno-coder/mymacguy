I
Open to All Bodies

No prior training required. We work with performers, non-performers, dancers, musicians, writers — anyone who wants to investigate presence and play.

II
Sliding Scale Always

Money should not be a barrier to practice. All workshops offer sliding scale pricing. If you can't afford even the minimum, reach out — we'll figure it out.

III
Process Over Product

These sessions are laboratories, not showcases. What matters is the investigation. Nothing we do here needs to be "good" — it needs to be honest.
