01 — About

We blur the lines
between performance
and reality.

I
Truth in Performance

We honor the truth, however difficult it may be to face. Our work does not offer easy answers — it offers honest questions.

II
New Modalities

We investigate the process of performance creation and aim to develop new modalities to achieve it — pushing beyond conventional theatrical forms.

III
Risk as Language

We value risk-taking to challenge audiences' perception of performance. The uncomfortable moment is where the real work begins.

IV
Total Theatre

We utilize all elements of theatrical storytelling in a dynamic way — sound, body, space, light, and silence are equally weighted instruments.

Misster Blue

CoHo Theatre

Utilizing the structure of Maurice Ravel's Bolero, the forms of Anne Adam's painting "Unraveling Bolero," and the aesthetics of Bowerbird mating rituals — Misster Blue was conceived as a journey of self-realization. Accompanied by sound created then distorted from an audio recording of a poem, the audience is invited to take a dharmic journey of repeated loop structures that eventually give way to discover a beauty in the repetition as each moment passes.

03 — The Ensemble

The People

Robert Augustine
Founder · Director · Deviser

Trained at Portland State University and PETE's Institute of Contemporary Performance. Expert in The Viewpoints, Suzuki Method, and Clown. His work explores removing the boundary between performance and reality — from Cambodian circus schools to Nevada City storytelling camps.

Amelia Whitney
Co-Founder · Performer · Creator

CalArts MFA. Performed at REDCAT, Elysian Theater, Will Geer's Theatricum Botanicum, Highways Performance Space. Trained in Grotowski, Viewpoints, Butoh, Clown, and Deep Listening. Writes, directs, and moves in strange ways.
