Policies & Terms

Store Policy

Fair, simple policies from Elena — because handmade should come with peace of mind.

Returns

Return Policy

Because every piece is handmade to order, we accept returns within 14 days of delivery for items that are unused and in their original condition.

To start a return, email Elena at picswelove2024@gmail.com with your order details. Return shipping costs are the buyer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Exchanges

Exchange Policy

We're happy to exchange items within 14 days of delivery. For jewelry, we can exchange for a different color or length (subject to availability). For pillows, we can exchange for a different color.

Contact Elena to arrange your exchange. If an exchange results in a price difference, we'll handle that adjustment via PayPal.

Damaged Items

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, Elena will make it right — at no additional cost to you. Please email within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the damage or incorrect item, and we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund.

Custom Orders

Custom & Special Orders

Elena loves custom work. You can request custom colors, lengths, or bead combinations for jewelry. Pillows can be made in custom fabric colors (subject to availability).

Custom orders are final sale — they cannot be returned or exchanged, since they are made specifically for you. Discuss your custom request with Elena before placing an order.

Payment

Payment & Privacy

All orders are placed via email inquiry. After you send your order, Elena will reply with secure payment instructions. We do not store your payment information.

Your personal information (name, email, shipping address) is used only to fulfill your order and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Questions about our policies?

Email Elena at picswelove2024@gmail.com — she's happy to help.
