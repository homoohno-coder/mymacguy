Elena's Story

Swiss roots,
American heart.

Born in Switzerland and proud of 44 years in the USA, Elena spent 24 years in real estate before following her true passion — travel and handcraft.

Fun Travel was born from a simple belief: your travel accessories should bring you joy, not anxiety. Every pillow and piece of jewelry is made to be affordable, beautiful, and light enough that losing a suitcase won't break your heart.

"I will produce reasonably priced travel items that you will not regret buying if a suitcase is lost, stolen or misplaced." — Elena
